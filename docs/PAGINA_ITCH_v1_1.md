# The Fifth Choice

**A free Quintessential Quintuplets fan visual novel. Name your own price: $0 is fine.**
*Available in English and Spanish. Texto en español más abajo.*

## English

Five sisters. One new tutor. A month that has to go well.

You take a tutoring job that pays far too much to turn down. Then you meet your students: identical quintuplets who are nothing alike, and who are not exactly thrilled to have you. The story starts where the original does and then goes its own way.

**Who will you go looking for, and how will you answer?**

### What's in this version (v1.1): Prologue + Chapter 1
- Choices that matter: they shape how each sister sees you, and how your job goes.
- Five sister events, three per playthrough, so every run leaves someone out.
- Minigames, one themed around each sister.
- A group chat, report cards, a progress screen and an Extras gallery.
- Play under your own name.
- English and Spanish: switch any time in Options.

Chapters 2 and 3 are still to come.
